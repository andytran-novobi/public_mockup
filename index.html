AP / Accounting
Suggested walkthrough — Start at the Share Wizard to see how AP invites a vendor. Then open the Vendor Intake Form, choose a tax form and a payment method to see both branches, and click Submit. That lands on the Participation form where Update Contact opens the updated Vendor Profile.
Not final UI — FDD states the real survey is built on Staging (per Summer); question layout/placement defers to that build. These mockups show intended content and behavior, not pixel-final screens. Sensitive tax IDs (TIN/SSN/EIN, DOB) and bank numbers are shown masked.
