Return a reel (R06, A-011) — A reel pulled at the end of FIFO goes back to the stock room. Record it with an internal transfer WH/SMT → WH/Stock: WH/SMT On Hand −2,500, WH/Stock +2,500.
If the return is not recorded — WH/SMT stays too high and WH/Stock too low, so Odoo may pick a reel that is not on the shelf. Machine losses (mis-picks, reel ends) are fixed by cycle counts of WH/SMT (A-012, INV-019).
