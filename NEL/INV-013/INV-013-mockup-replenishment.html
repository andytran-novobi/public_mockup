Rules on WH/SMT, route SMT Top-up (R05, D-008) — One rule per part, for the parts that stay loaded in the machines (Tessa's high-runner sheet). The SMT Top-up route has its own transfer type, so top-ups are listed apart from MO picks.
Example (BR-005) — Forecast = On Hand + incoming − demand from MOs that take the part from WH/SMT: 1,149 − 300 (WH/MO/10095: 100 boards × 3) = 849. That is below Min 2,000, so Odoo orders up to Max: 9,000 − 849 = 8,151, rounded down to full multiples of 3,000 = 6,000. Trigger Auto creates the top-up at once.
Max ≥ Min + one multiple (FDD §9) — If Max is lower than Min + Multiple Quantity, standard rounding can order 0. There is no system guard; the set-up checklist covers it (FDD Section 9, step 7).
NEL sends the list later (Q-005, closed) — The values on this screen are examples. When NEL sends its Min/Max list, Novobi loads the rules. Until then, SMT parts are picked per MO only.
Pick and top-up together (standard, FDD §5 note) — A part with a rule that is also short for an MO gets both a pick (missing quantity) and a top-up (up to Max). WH/SMT may end above Max; accepted on 9 Nov 2026.
