INV-013 Option A: one SMT location and one SMT work center

Both MyData machines share one stock location, WH/SMT, and one work center, MyData Pick and Place. Parts consumed at the MyData step come from WH/SMT. All other parts of the MO still come from WH/WIP-Pre-Production. Source: INV-013 FDD v0.5 (short) and v0.4, 9 Nov 2026.

Example used on every screen
100pcs of capacitor 191-B0402102K-1 needed by WH/MO/10095 (100 × W8CB-1, 1 per board)
90pcs in WH/SMT: 80 on MyData 1 + 10 on MyData 2. Odoo sees only the total.
90pcs reserved in WH/SMT when the planner confirms the MO
10pcs picked from WH/Stock into WH/SMT (Pick Components WH/PC/00180)
