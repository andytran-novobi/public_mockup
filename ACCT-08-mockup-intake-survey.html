Conditional fields (R12 / R13 / R10) — The sign-on-behalf and Content Creator gates reveal (and require) their fields only on Yes, via native triggering answers; the required, multi-select Payment method reveals a separate block per selected method the same way — here Wire + ACH are both on, so both blocks show (D-008/D-011/D-015). Tax forms are a mandatory 3-way selector revealing exactly one required upload — W-9 / W-8BEN / W-8BEN-E (R14 / BR-018 / BR-019 / D-016); the upload question type comes from the third-party app bi_survey_files_upload, not custom code (D-018). The tax choices are single-select; the payment branch tabs toggle each block independently as a mockup aid.
Sensitive PII (Q-001) — Structured tax IDs are no longer typed as answers; the uploaded W-9 / W-8BEN / W-8BEN-E PDF holds SSN/EIN, ITIN, foreign TIN and date of birth. Bank routing/account numbers are still captured as answers. Restrict access to submissions and attachments. Bank answers are never auto-written to a bank record — AP keys the validated account (D-008). On Update Contact an answers PDF plus the uploaded tax form are filed into a restricted section of the vendor's Invoicing tab — readable only at Bookkeeper level and above, and deliberately not in chatter, which anyone who can read the vendor could see (R16 / D-019).
Submit (R01 / R09 / R11) — The form is reached only via a personal invite link; the submission is already linked to the invited contact. On completion of a non-test entry, Accounting is emailed a link to the Participation record. Click Submit to jump to that Participation form.
