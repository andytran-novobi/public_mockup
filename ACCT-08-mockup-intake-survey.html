Conditional branch (R02) — The tax-form choice reveals only its own questions via native triggering answers. W-9 and W-8BEN are never shown together to the vendor. The two tabs above are a mockup aid to preview both.
Sensitive PII (Q-001) — TIN/SSN/EIN, ITIN, foreign TIN and Date of birth are captured as plain survey answers. Access to these should be restricted. Values are shown masked in this mockup.
Submit (R01 / R09) — Anyone with the link can submit anonymously. On completion of a non-test entry, Accounting is emailed a link to the Participation record. Click Submit to jump to that Participation form.
