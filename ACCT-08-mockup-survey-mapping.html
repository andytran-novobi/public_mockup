Rows left blank in the Contact field column are not written to the vendor (e.g. Vendor type, Tax form, and all bank-detail questions — the bank answers stay on the submission and AP keys the verified bank account manually, D-008).
No-code mapping (R07) — Turn on Update contact from submissions to reveal the Contact field column. Pick a target vendor field per question from the tree — no developer change. Update Contact on each submission writes each answer to its mapped field.
Export-style picker — The field tree mirrors Odoo's Export dialog: search + expandable, relational drill-down (Country ▸ Name). Only text-like fields and Country/State (matched by name) are selectable in v1; other relational/selection targets are greyed (D-007).
Guards — If no question maps to a contact field, Update writes nothing (BR-013). A Country/State value that can't be matched by name is left blank with a note (BR-012). Bank questions stay unmapped by design — never written to a bank record (D-008).
Presentation note (D-006 / D-009) — FDD v0.3 confirms the survey-level toggle shown here (Update contact from submissions, D-009) and keeps the mapping itself per question (each question's Options tab, D-006). This consolidated table is an illustrative overview of those per-question settings, not a separate config screen.
